Analyst notes

How to interpret this dashboard

01
Location ≠ service coverage

Map markers indicate approximate suburb, street or landmark locations, not confirmed MSAN cabinets or subscriber footprints.

02
Traffic ≠ data volume

Sum of monthly average Mbps rates across MSAN links does not represent monthly traffic transferred in GB.

03
Compare carefully

Population changes are model-generated. Shared trends cannot establish population as the cause of changing utilisation.

TRANSPARENCY · REPRODUCIBILITY

Data & methodology.

What this demonstration uses, what it does not prove, and how to interpret results.

ACADEMIC RESEARCH PROTOTYPE
⌁

Network measurements

50 synthetic MSAN time series aggregated from daily source records to 3,000 monthly MSAN rows. Inbound and outbound values are rates (Mbps), and peak utilisation is a percentage.

SYNTHETIC
♙

Population measurements

600 constituency-month population estimates across ten model areas. These are generated projections informed by census anchors, not directly observed monthly headcounts.

MODELLED
⌖

Location reference points

50 approximate Windhoek place references mapped to nine candidate constituencies. A landmark substituted for one synthetic entry. No point confirms telecom cabinet positions or subscribers served.

PROVISIONAL
▧

Boundary geography

Ten Khomas constituency polygons from the US Census Bureau September 2023 Namibia ADM2 boundary file. Administrative-name reconciliations are provisional.

2023 REFERENCE

Forecast and interpretation rules

Prediction target: mean daily-peak inbound utilisation, averaged across the synthetic MSANs assigned to a constituency. It is not the maximum network-wide utilisation.

Regression: simple linear trend on 60 monthly points, plus monthly seasonal offsets. Optional annual adjustment modifies the fitted baseline scenario. Values are limited to the physically meaningful 0–100% range.

Limitations: no validation against genuine telecom traffic, verified site/service boundaries, independently observed monthly population, engineering upgrade history or network subscriber forecasts. Treat higher-year forecasts as speculative.

Power BI: the website's native graphs are generated from the bundled project datasets; The Analysis & Findings page calculates its results locally from the bundled datasets; no Power BI sign-in or network reporting service is required.
